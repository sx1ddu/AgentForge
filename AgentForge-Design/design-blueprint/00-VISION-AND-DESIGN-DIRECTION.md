# AgentForge — The Forge

## Design thesis
AgentForge should feel like a **workbench for directing intelligent work**, not a chat product with agent badges. The central metaphor is a precision engineering studio: users submit a brief, choose or delegate to a specialist, inspect the work, and collect the result. The visual language is editorial, technical and calm. High information density is allowed where it improves control; visual noise is not.

## Brand character
- **Precise:** aligned geometry, strong typographic hierarchy, restrained borders.
- **Capable:** execution state, agent responsibility and output provenance are visible.
- **Human-readable:** technical mechanisms are translated into clear language.
- **Confident:** restrained color, no spectacle masquerading as functionality.

## Chosen direction: Graphite / Signal / Paper
Dark mode is the primary product environment: near-black graphite surfaces, warm neutral text, and a sharp acid-lime signal accent. Light mode uses warm paper surfaces, ink typography and the same accent. A small set of semantic colors identifies execution state, not arbitrary agent personalities.

## Signature visual device
A **work rail**: a thin, numbered vertical execution spine that can show the confirmed sequence of a workflow. It becomes the signature of the product across landing demonstrations, multi-agent execution and historical run summaries. It must only show stages known from the response or persisted result; no invented live telemetry.

## Product positioning
**Direct the work. Inspect the thinking. Keep the output.**
AgentForge brings specialist AI agents into one workspace for research, coding, document creation and collaborative engineering tasks.

## Experience principles
1. The task is the primary object; the chat transcript is one representation of the task.
2. Agent selection is understandable and reversible before sending.
3. Every generated artifact has a clear path to inspect, copy or download.
4. Costs are visible before commitment where the product can determine them; otherwise show the documented rate and avoid false estimates.
5. Distinguish confirmed execution from illustrative process diagrams.
6. Empty states teach through useful actions, not generic encouragement.
7. Desktop supports parallel context; mobile prioritizes the brief, response and output.

## Anti-patterns rejected
No purple-blue gradient, glass panels, floating orbs, repeated bento cards, fake live terminal, fake activity feed, decorative charts, excessive pills, or ungrounded claims such as verified code, real-time streaming, persistent projects or team collaboration between human users.

## Visual benchmark
The intended quality bar is the calm hierarchy and craft of Linear/Raycast/Stripe—not copied layouts. The application should be recognizable by its warm graphite palette, signal-lime action language, work rail and crisp mono metadata.
